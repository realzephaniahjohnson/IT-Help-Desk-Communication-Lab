# Account and Password Support

Templates for common account-related requests.

---

## Password Reset

I've reset your password.

Please sign in using the temporary password, then create a new one when prompted.

Let me know if you run into any issues.

---

## Account Locked

Your account was locked due to multiple unsuccessful sign-in attempts.

I've unlocked it.

Please try signing in again.

---

## MFA Issue

Let's make sure we're looking at the right issue.

Do you still have access to the phone or authenticator app that's connected to your account?

---

## MFA Reset

I've reset your multi-factor authentication.

The next time you sign in, you'll be asked to set it up again.

---

## Verifying Identity

Before I make changes to your account, I need to verify your identity.

Could you please confirm your full name and employee ID?

---

## Permission Issue

I'm checking your account permissions now.

I'll let you know if anything looks incorrect.

---

## Account Access Restored

Everything looks good now.

Please sign out completely, sign back in, and test it one more time.
