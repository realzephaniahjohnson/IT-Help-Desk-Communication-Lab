# Escalation Scripts

Sometimes an issue needs to be handed off to another team.

These templates help keep users informed.

---

## Escalating to Tier 2

I'm escalating your ticket to our Tier 2 support team.

They'll review everything that's already been completed so you won't have to repeat yourself.

---

## Network Team

This appears to be related to the network rather than your computer.

I'm forwarding this to our network team for further investigation.

## Security Team

Because this involves account security, I'm escalating the ticket to our security team.

They'll continue from here and reach out if they need any additional information.

---

## Application Support

The issue appears to be with the application itself rather than your device.

I'm sending this over to the application support team.

---
