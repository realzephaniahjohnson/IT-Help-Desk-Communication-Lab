# Ticket Closure

Templates for wrapping up support requests professionally.

---

## Asking the User to Test

I've completed the changes on my end.

Could you test it one more time and let me know if everything is working?

---

## Confirming Resolution

Great!

I'm glad to hear everything is working again.

---

## Closing the Ticket

Since the issue has been resolved, I'll go ahead and close this ticket.

If anything comes up again, don't hesitate to contact the IT Help Desk.

---

## Follow-Up Email

Hello,

Just following up to confirm your issue has been resolved.

If everything is working as expected, I'll close the ticket.

If not, just reply to this email and we'll continue troubleshooting.

Thanks,

Zephaniah

---

## Closing Due to No Response

Hello,

I've tried reaching out a couple of times but haven't heard back.

I'll go ahead and close the ticket for now.

If you still need assistance, simply reply or open a new ticket and we'll be happy to help.
