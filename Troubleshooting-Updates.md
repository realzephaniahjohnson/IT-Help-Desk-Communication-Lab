# Troubleshooting Updates

These are communication templates I use to keep users informed while I troubleshoot their issue.

---

## Letting the User Know I'm Looking Into It

Thanks for the information.

I'm going to look into this now. I'll keep you updated as I work through it.

---

## Gathering More Information

I have a few more questions that will help narrow down the cause.

- When did this start?
- Does it happen every time?
- Is anyone else around you experiencing the same issue?

---

## Reviewing the Issue

I'm checking a few things on my end to see what's causing the problem.

This should only take a few minutes.

---

## Running Diagnostics

I'm running a few diagnostic checks on your computer.

I'll let you know what I find.

---

## Checking for Service Outages

I'm checking to see if this is a known issue affecting other users.

---

## Requesting Remote Access

If you're okay with it, I'd like to remotely connect to your computer.

That will let me investigate the issue more quickly.

Please save any open work before we begin.

---

## Restart Required

The next troubleshooting step requires a restart.

Please save anything you're working on first.

Let me know once the computer has finished restarting.

---

## Waiting for Results

Go ahead and try the task again.

Let me know exactly what happens this time, even if the behavior changes slightly.

---

## If the Issue Isn't Fixed

Thanks for testing that.

That tells me we've ruled out one possible cause, so I'll continue troubleshooting.

---

## Keeping the User Updated

Just wanted to give you a quick update.

I'm still investigating the issue, and I appreciate your patience.

I'll keep you posted as soon as I have more information.
